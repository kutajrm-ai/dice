About DICE

A Nigeria where every citizen — regardless of gender, age, disability or background — is informed, empowered, and takes part in free, fair, credible and inclusive elections.

Registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission on 7 March 2025, DICE serves as watchdog, educator and advocate at every phase of the electoral cycle — through citizen education, non-partisan observation, community engagement, advocacy and capacity building.

What we do

Seven programme areas, one goal.

01

Election Observation

INEC-accredited domestic observation of every phase of the electoral process.

02

Voter & Civic Education

Electoral rights, media literacy, and countering misinformation and hate speech.

03

Women’s Participation

Advocating 50% gender parity and mentoring women seeking office.

04

Youth Leadership

Youth electoral clubs, campus programmes and a volunteer observer corps.

05

Disability & Inclusion

Accessible polling units and participation for IDPs and marginalised communities.

06

Advocacy & Research

Election analysis and evidence for legal and policy reform.

07

Capacity Building

Training observers, civic educators and partner organisations.
