Five steps to your ballot in 2027

Tap a step for the full guide.

  1. Register or update your detailsAt an INEC CVR centre, or pre-register online.
  2. Collect your PVCIn person, from your INEC LGA office or designated centre.
  3. Know your polling unitYou can only vote where you registered.
  4. Learn how voting worksAccreditation with BVAS, thumb-printing, your rights, assistance for PWDs.
  5. Vote and protect the countVote early, refuse inducements, report incidents — not rumours.

Dates as set in INEC’s timetable for the 2027 general elections. Non-partisan. Only INEC conducts elections and declares results.
